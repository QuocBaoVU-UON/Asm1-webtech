Our History

TuneTown was created to provide music lovers with a simple and welcoming place to discover albums from different genres. The website began with a small collection of popular jazz and classical music and has continued to develop into a broader music collection.

Our goal is to make discovering music enjoyable and easy. TuneTown brings together album information, artists, descriptions and related resources so visitors can explore music and learn more about the albums they enjoy.
